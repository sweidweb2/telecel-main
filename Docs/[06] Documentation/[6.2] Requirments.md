## RFQ — ASIP Website Design & Development

**Issued by:** Telecel Group
**Reference:** TG-ASIP-WEB-20260729
**Issue date:** 29 July 2026
**Target go-live:** 30 August 2026
**Status:** Draft for procurement issue
**Confidentiality:** For invited vendors only 

### 1. RFQ Overview

**Procurement intent:**
Telecel is seeking a capable web partner to deliver a lean, premium and launch-ready ASIP website. The scope is intentionally focused on an effective corporate and portfolio platform, not a complex custom application.

**Background:**
The Africa Startup Initiative Program (ASIP) is a Telecel Group initiative established to support African technology ventures through acceleration, ecosystem access and market visibility. Telecel is relaunching the website to present the programme’s history, portfolio, impact and next chapter through a modern, credible and easy-to-manage platform.

**Project objectives:**

* Replace the outdated website with a clear, modern and mobile-first experience.
* Present ASIP’s programme, portfolio, impact, partners and historical archive through verified content.
* Provide a simple CMS that Telecel can maintain internally.
* Launch Phase 1 by **30 August 2026**.

**RFQ at a glance:**

* Delivery model: Fixed-fee, fast-track Phase 1 build
* Launch language: English; French-ready structure only
* Content: Approved sitemap and core copy supplied by Telecel
* Platform: Mature, non-proprietary CMS such as WordPress or equivalent
* Primary audience: Founders, partners, investors, media and ecosystem stakeholders

## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 UX and visual design

* Confirm supplied information architecture and translate it into clear user journeys.
* Create a premium ASIP visual system aligned with Telecel brand standards and existing ASIP identity.
* Design homepage and required templates for desktop, tablet and mobile.
* Use light, purposeful motion; avoid heavy effects that slow the site or increase maintenance.
* Develop a short looping hero treatment using existing approved footage or imagery.
* No new production shoot required.

### 2.2 Website build

* Responsive frontend development and CMS configuration.
* Core pages:

  * Home
  * About
  * Programme
  * Portfolio
  * Impact
  * Partners
  * Insights
  * Register Interest / Apply
  * Contact
  * FAQ
* Portfolio structure with simple filters such as:

  * Cohort
  * Country
  * Sector
* Reusable templates for:

  * Startup profiles
  * Articles
  * Updates
  * Case studies
* Simple forms routed to Telecel-designated email addresses, with consent messaging.
* Basic search, navigation, footer links and social links.
* Content population using Telecel-supplied copy and approved assets.

### 2.3 Technical setup

* SEO fundamentals:

  * Editable metadata
  * Clean URLs
  * Sitemap
  * Redirects
  * Social-sharing fields
* Analytics using Telecel's selected standard analytics account.
* Media optimization for mobile and constrained bandwidth.
* Standard security hardening.
* Role-based CMS access.
* Backups.
* Form-spam protection.
* Deployment to Telecel-confirmed hosting environment.
* Testing across current mainstream browsers and priority mobile devices.

# 3. Essential Requirements & Scope Boundaries

### 3.1 Acceptance requirements

| Area        | Minimum requirement                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Design      | Approved homepage and consistent template system aligned with Telecel and ASIP identities                     |
| Mobile      | Fully responsive and easy to use on common mobile screen sizes                                                |
| CMS         | Telecel administrators can edit pages, portfolio entries, articles, forms and metadata without vendor support |
| Portfolio   | Startup profiles and basic filtering work correctly and remain easy to expand                                 |
| Performance | Optimized images/video, lightweight pages and practical performance on standard mobile connections            |
| Quality     | No critical functional defects, broken links, placeholder content or incomplete pages at launch               |
| Handover    | Admin training, credentials, source files, licenses and concise documentation delivered                       |

### 3.2 Scope boundaries

**Included in Phase 1:**

* Core website design and development
* Standard CMS configuration
* Portfolio archive and simple filters
* Content loading and launch support
* Existing-asset hero motion
* Basic SEO, analytics and forms
* Training
* 30-day bug-fix warranty

**Excluded / quote separately:**

* New photography or video production
* Founder or investor portals
* Mobile application development
* Custom CRM or complex third-party integrations
* French translation or full multilingual population
* Ongoing copywriting, social media or campaign management
* Paid hosting
* Third-party subscriptions or licenses

**Cost-control principle:**
Vendors should use standard, proven CMS components where practical and avoid unnecessary bespoke development. Optional enhancements must be priced separately and must not affect the Phase 1 launch.

# 7. Evaluation, Commercial Terms & Handover

### Evaluation criteria

### Commercial and contractual principles

* Selected vendor will be appointed under Telecel's standard procurement and contractual terms.
* Proposal should be fixed-fee for the stated Phase 1 scope.
* Optional items must be clearly separated.
* Payment milestones linked to accepted deliverables and final handover.
* All approved source files, website code, credentials, design files and content structures transferred to Telecel at completion.
* All third-party tools, themes, plugins, licenses and recurring costs disclosed before appointment.
* Vendor must keep Telecel and ASIP information confidential.
* Work may not be published without written approval.
* Telecel may amend, suspend or cancel the RFQ without obligation to appoint. 

### Final handover checklist

* Live website and verified domain deployment.
* Administrator accounts and access credentials.
* Source code, design files and license register.
* Backups and recovery instructions.
* CMS training for nominated Telecel users.
* Concise technical and content-management guide.
* 30-day launch warranty and support contact.
